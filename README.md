## About the Project

This project is developed as part of the National Children's Science Congress (NCSC) under the theme:

> **Lifestyle for Environment and Sustainability**

The project focuses on the application of **Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS)** for sustainability, particularly through the study of traditional knowledge associated with medicinal plants.

The selected plant for this study is:

### Leucas aspera

| Classification | Details |
|---|---|
| Common Name | Thumbai / Guma |
| Scientific Name | *Leucas aspera* |
| Family | Lamiaceae |
| Kingdom | Plantae |
| Growth Form | Herb |
| Habitat | Grasslands, roadsides, open fields and disturbed areas |
| Research Area | Traditional knowledge, medicinal plants and conservation |

The study attempts to understand how traditional knowledge surrounding *Leucas aspera* can be documented, scientifically examined and connected with modern approaches to environmental sustainability.

---

## Research Question

Traditional knowledge about medicinal plants has been passed through generations, but modernization, habitat changes and the decline of traditional practices may threaten both the knowledge and the plants associated with it.

This project investigates:

> **How can traditional knowledge associated with Leucas aspera be documented, scientifically understood and transformed into practical community-based strategies for its sustainable conservation and responsible utilization?**

---

## Objectives

The major objectives of this research are:

1. To document traditional knowledge associated with *Leucas aspera*.

2. To study the distribution and availability of the plant in selected areas.

3. To investigate how knowledge about the plant varies between different generations.

4. To understand the traditionally reported uses of the plant.

5. To compare traditional claims with available scientific literature.

6. To study simple observable and measurable characteristics of the plant.

7. To identify factors that may affect the availability and conservation of the plant.

8. To develop practical and community-oriented conservation strategies.

9. To promote responsible cultivation and utilization rather than uncontrolled harvesting.

10. To demonstrate how Indian traditional knowledge can contribute to sustainable environmental practices.

---

## Why Leucas aspera?

*Leucas aspera* is a widespread herb found in many parts of India and has a long association with traditional knowledge.

Its relatively common occurrence makes it suitable for a student-level field study involving:

- Field observation
- Plant identification
- Distribution surveys
- Traditional knowledge surveys
- Interviews
- Questionnaire-based data collection
- Documentation
- Basic scientific observations
- Community-based conservation planning

The project does not assume that traditional medicinal claims automatically constitute scientifically proven treatments. Instead, traditional claims are documented and compared with available scientific evidence.
